KALMAN CINEMA · Technical Q&A for Judges
1. Why use a Kalman filter instead of asking an LLM for anomaly detection?
Latency & Mathematical Precision: Live 4K broadcast telemetry ticks at 50Hz across 5 geographic regions. Querying an LLM on every tick incurs 2–4 seconds of latency and thousands of dollars in token bills. The scalar Kalman 1D filter calculates the Normalized Innovation Squared (NIS) in <0.05 milliseconds with zero token cost. Gemini is called strictly when a statistical gate is breached, achieving instantaneous anomaly detection with zero hallucination.
2. How is Google Gemini Pro actually integrated in the live loop?
When Kalman flags an innovation breach (e.g., z > 3.5), the Diagnostician Agent activates. It correlates multi-signal residuals, queries the Grafana MCP endpoint for regional gateway logs, and executes Gemini Pro (via Vertex AI ADK) to synthesize root-cause hypotheses with confidence scores and query citations.

They are interactive kinetic representations of living autonomous subsystems:

  • Scribble: Scribe Owl, authoring immutable postmortems with 100% cryptographic query citations.
  • Ignis: Chaos Dragon, summoned to execute authorized destructive failovers with cinematic fire.
4. How does Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Governance protect the system?
Low-risk actions (e.g. shifting 25% traffic between healthy CDNs) execute autonomously. High-risk destructive actions (e.g. severing a primary origin cluster) are strictly held by the Governor. A human NOC operator must review the blast radius and manually approve the action—or awaken Ignis in the Caldera to execute it.
